How it works

Three steps, and you never touch an editing app.

  1. Step 1

    Upload

    Tell us what you make, where you post and how you like it to look, then send your footage.

  2. Step 2

    Matched

    We pick the editor in our network who fits your style, platform and budget, and agree the price with you first.

  3. Step 3

    Delivered

    You review a draft, ask for changes, and get the finished file, usually within 48 hours.

How payment works: you pay a deposit before work starts, approve a watermarked draft, then pay the balance and receive the clean file.
