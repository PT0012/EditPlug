Privacy notice

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Who we are

EditPlug is a video-editing service run in the UK by [YOUR LEGAL NAME], a sole trader, who is the "controller" of your personal information. Contact: editplugteam@gmail.com.

Your EditPlug account (used for editing projects and EditPlug AI Studio) is run on our own server at aistudio.editplug.com. The AI Studio privacy notice also covers your account and anything you make in the studio.

What we collect and why

  • Sign-up and quote forms. When you fill in our forms (run by Tally) we get what you type, such as your name, email, social handles, the content you make or your editing portfolio and rates. We use it to reply, match creators with editors, and quote and deliver work. Legal basis: taking steps to enter into a contract with you, and our legitimate interest in running the service.
  • Your EditPlug account and project briefs. Your name, email, password (stored scrambled, never readable) and the briefs, quotes and project stages for your edits. We use them to quote and deliver work and keep you updated by email. Legal basis: contract.
  • Visit counts, without cookies. Our own server counts page views, where visitors came from (the referring site only) and which main buttons are clicked, as daily totals. Nothing is stored on your device and no IP address or identifier is kept, so this runs for everyone. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing whether the site works.
  • Emails and messages you send us by email or social media, to answer you. Legal basis: legitimate interests, or contract.
  • Footage and files you send for editing, used only to do the job and shared only with the editor working on it. Legal basis: contract.
  • Analytics, only if you accept. Google Analytics and Contentsquare record how the site is used (pages viewed, clicks, device and approximate location, and in Contentsquare's case anonymised session recordings). They set cookies, so they only run after you click "Accept". Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through .

Cookies and storage

Without your consent this site sets no analytics cookies. If you sign in to your EditPlug account, our account server sets the cookies needed to keep you signed in, plus ep_signed_in (just "1", no identity, up to 14 days) so this site can show "My account" instead of "Sign in". These are strictly necessary for the service you asked for. We store your cookie choice in your browser's local storage so we don't ask again. If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies named _ga and _ga_* (up to 2 years), and Contentsquare sets cookies beginning _cs_ (up to 13 months).

Who we share it with

  • The editor (or client) on your job, only what they need to do the work. Some editors are based outside the UK.
  • Service providers who process data for us: Tally (forms), Google (email, file storage and analytics), Contentsquare (analytics), Notion (our records), GitHub (website hosting), Oracle Cloud in London (our account server) and Resend (account emails).

Where data goes outside the UK, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or the providers' standard contractual safeguards. We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

Enquiries and sign-ups that don't lead to work: up to 2 years, then deleted. Client and editor records linked to paid work: 6 years after the last job, for tax and accounting. Footage: deleted within 90 days of delivery unless you ask us to keep it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, or take it elsewhere. Email us and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk (opens in a new tab).
